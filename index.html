What the data found.

The patterns that held up across both leagues, and the ones that did not.

Counter-attacks · holds in both leagues

Bodies behind the ball decide counter-attacks.

When few defenders were goal-side at the moment the ball was lost, far more counter-attacks ended in a shot, and the chances were about 7 times better in quality.

Passing lanes

Count the defenders, not just yes or no.

Each defender standing in a passing lane lowers the chance the pass arrives. Which way they face barely matters.

Not every pattern is universal.

Pressing with lots of players in build-up and the midfield gap mattered in the A-League, but not in the Bundesliga. Set pieces did not depend on shape in either.
